Desk

Basic marketing needs, from evidence

DESK is the beginner layer on top of Deep Analyze. It maps what we already collected onto the eight questions a marketer actually asks. Empty slots stay unavailable. We will not invent traffic, TAM, demographics, or Instagram Insights.

How to read a chip

ObservedCopied from a public page or official API.
CalculatedDerived from those public numbers — a median, a cadence, a score.
ModelledA labelled score on public fields. Not a platform fact.
UnavailableWe did not see it. Empty is not zero.

Playbooks

A playbook is a four-week template that appears only when a public surface triggered it — a feed, ads.txt, llms.txt, a login wall, a printed CIK/CIN, or a missing offer. It is not a forecast and not a media plan. Compare two stored desks on Compare without declaring a winner. Optional prose appears only when an LLM key is configured; the desk works with none.

Owned feedIf they advertised RSS/Atom, publish offer → proof → a missing path → re-measure.
ads.txtA publisher declaration. Not spend, not your paid-social brief.
llms.txt / ai.txtA crawl invitation. Not bot traffic and not a ranking.
Login wallEmpty is not zero. Paste a YouTube or site URL they already print.
